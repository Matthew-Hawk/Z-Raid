Survival field manual

Choose a survivor for your entire run. Select a location, weapon and gear before every raid. Survive five Yes/No encounters to recover one cure fragment. Three fragments save the world.

Combat helps you fight and force entry. Stealth helps you hide and navigate. Scavenging helps you recover supplies. Items modify these checks; drawbacks matter.

Encounter outcomes depend on your survivor, equipment, supplies, location hazards and raid difficulty. Checks are rolled behind the scenes. Stronger stats help, but never guarantee survival. Loud routes raise pursuit even on success; quiet routes lower it. Failures also attract infected. Higher pursuit makes later encounters more dangerous. Pursuit clears at camp.

Supplies add morale and are consumed at camp: recover 8 health plus 2 per supply (maximum 6 supplies), capped at maximum health. Every successful raid upgrades all eight items by +1 in their specialty, while hazards grow faster. Health carries through encounters. Zero health ends your run immediately and clears progress.

Use Tab and Enter/Space, a mouse, or touch. Both Yes and No describe an action and have risks. CRT is optional. Runs are not saved when you close or reload.
